Changed the module to require Jahia 8.2.2.0, app-shell 4.0 and jContent 3.8.1 or later. Since 3.3.0, its actions do not show on an earlier app-shell; the module now refuses to start there instead. Upgrade Jahia, app-shell and jContent before you upgrade this module.
